ASCEND and your health data

ASCEND reads three things from Health Connect, and only after you allow it: your steps, your sleep and your workouts. It uses them to fill in your Walk, Sleep and Workout quests and your Body log.

ASCEND never writes to Health Connect, never sells or shares your data, and has no ads or tracking. The numbers are stored on this phone. If you turn on the optional sync account, your ASCEND data (including these daily totals) is copied to your own private sync space so it also appears on your laptop; nobody else can read it.

You can stop at any time: switch Health Connect off in ASCEND's Settings, or remove ASCEND's access in the Health Connect app.

ASCEND is a personal app built for one person.
